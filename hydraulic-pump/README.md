# Hydraulic Pump Assembly — Functional & Exploded Views

Mechanical assembly model of a hydraulic pump developed in SolidWorks, focused on
component integration, functional layout, and clear visualization of internal parts.

## Scope of Work
- Assembly of multiple mechanical components into a functional hydraulic system
- Definition of component relationships and mating conditions
- Visualization of internal architecture and part interaction

## Assembly Views
- Functional Assembly View – Complete pump assembly showing overall geometry and
  component arrangement
- Exploded Assembly View – Separated view illustrating all individual components and
  their spatial relationships within the assembly

## Components
- Pump housing
- Shaft and rotating elements
- Internal mechanical components
- Fasteners and structural elements

## Tools Used
- SolidWorks (Assemblies and Drawings)

## Objective
Demonstrate proficiency in mechanical assembly modeling, understanding of component
integration, and ability to communicate complex mechanical systems through functional
and exploded views.
