# Prompt-to-CAD | Engineering Portfolio Entry

**Category:** Mechanical Design / CAD Automation / AI-Assisted Engineering

**Summary:** Developed a prototype demonstrating conversion of natural-language mechanical requirements into interactive 3D mechanism concepts, with adjustable dimensions, iterative refinements, and CAD export options.

**Examples in the recorded demonstration:** Planetary gearsets, rack-and-pinion assemblies, and threaded hardware.

**Engineering motivation:** Investigate whether company-specific AI interfaces can accelerate repetitive preliminary modeling and design iteration without replacing the engineering validation process.

**What remains to validate:** Repeatability, dimensional fidelity, mechanism behavior, neutral CAD export integrity, time savings, and robustness against invalid requirements.

## Résumé bullet

- Built an AI-assisted Prompt-to-CAD prototype that interprets natural-language mechanical specifications and demonstrates interactive parametric 3D models, iterative design refinement, and CAD/mesh export workflows.
